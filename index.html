THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Brands spend millions in other people's stores. Few can say which of it sells.

Promoters, fixtures, POS and in-store digital still take a large share of the budget in third-party retail. After years of moving money to digital, most brands can no longer say which of those touchpoints, in which stores, actually drive sell-out.

MEY helps brands and retailers find out, and put the money where it works.

How we work

Three steps, and you can start at any of them.

01 · FIND THE GAPS

Diagnostic

A fixed-scope review of where your retail execution falls short of your strategy, from store and location choices to field teams, in-store spend and customer experience. It ends with the gaps ranked by value and clear next steps.

02 · CLOSE THEM

Project

A defined plan to close the gaps that matter most, with agreed deliverables: a UK launch plan, an operations redesign, a reallocation of in-store budget or a manufacturer shortlist.

03 · PROVE IT

Advisory

An ongoing retainer to drive delivery, measure what changed and keep the plan on course, with a senior retail adviser on hand for your leadership team.
